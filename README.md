# Med-Slice-Filler
Bridging the critical gaps in 3D medical imaging by reconstructing missing spatial slices with high-fidelity generative AI.

Hardware limitations and patient movement often leave spatial gaps in MRI and CT scans. Missing slices disrupt volumetric continuity, significantly hindering accurate medical diagnosis and high-resolution 3D modeling.
